How to install通用安装步骤

  1. Double-click the .iso to mount it — it appears as a disk on your Desktop.
  2. Inside, drag the app into the Applications folder. If you see a .dmg or .pkg instead, open that and follow its steps.
  3. First launch: right-click the app → Open to get past Gatekeeper.
  4. Eject the mounted disk when you are done.
  1. 双击 .iso 挂载——桌面会出现一个磁盘。
  2. 把里面的 App 拖进"应用程序"文件夹。若里面是 .dmg 或 .pkg，则打开它再按提示走。
  3. 首次打开：右键点 App →"打开"，绕过 Gatekeeper。
  4. 装完后推出已挂载的磁盘。

If an app "is damaged and can't be opened"若提示 App"已损坏，无法打开"

That is just Gatekeeper quarantine on downloaded files, not real damage. Open Terminal and run, then reopen the app:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/AppName.app"

那只是下载文件被 Gatekeeper 隔离，并非真损坏。打开"终端"运行下面命令，再重新打开 App：
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/应用名.app"
